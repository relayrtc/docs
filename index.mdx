## Choose your starting point

<ReferenceCards />

Use [installation](/installation) to choose your SDKs. To run the services yourself, start with [self-hosting](/self-hosting).

## Your first room

Your backend creates a room and issues one participant token per caller. Each browser joins with its token, then enables devices and receives the other participants' media.

The [first-call guide](/quickstart) covers that complete flow, including capture controls, local preview, remote video/audio and blocked autoplay.

## Build calls

- [Camera and microphone](/guides/camera-and-microphone): capture, preview, mute and change devices.
- [Screen sharing](/guides/screen-sharing): publish a display and optional display audio.
- [Audio-only roles](/guides/audio-only-rooms): distinguish speaker and listener authority.
- [Messages and events](/guides/messages-and-events): chat, reactions and participant metadata.
- [Connection recovery](/guides/connection-recovery): reconnect and renew authorized sessions.
- [Video quality](/guides/video-quality) and [TURN](/guides/turn-configuration): adapt media and inspect connectivity.

## Live streaming

[Streaming guides](/guides/live-streaming) cover ROOM, WHIP and RTMP sources, broadcast runs, private playback, recordings, clips, DVR and composition. Follow each feature's package and deployment requirements; current source interfaces can be newer than the published SDK baseline.

## Integrate your backend

Use [webhooks](/guides/webhooks) to receive signed resource events and [usage reports](/guides/usage) to inspect participant time and service traffic. Keep management keys on your server and authorize application users before issuing room tokens or playback grants.

## Self-hosting

The [deployment guide](/self-hosting) covers the service topology and configuration. For connection or playback problems, start with [troubleshooting calls](/guides/troubleshooting).
